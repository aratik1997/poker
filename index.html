      Game Rules — Texas Hold'em

      Poker Night plays standard No-Limit Texas Hold'em, the same core rules used at home games and casinos worldwide, adapted for 2–8 players.

      Objective

      Make the best 5-card hand using any combination of your 2 private hole cards and the 5 shared community cards, or make everyone else fold. Chips are the score — the player with the most chips is winning.

      Hand rankings (best to worst)

      1. Straight Flush — five sequential cards, same suit
      2. Four of a Kind — four cards of the same rank
      3. Full House — three of a kind + a pair
      4. Flush — five cards of the same suit
      5. Straight — five sequential cards, any suits
      6. Three of a Kind
      7. Two Pair
      8. One Pair
      9. High Card

      How a hand plays out

      Blinds: each hand, one seat posts the small blind and the next posts the big blind — forced bets that start the pot.
      Hole cards: every player is dealt 2 private cards.
      Pre-flop betting: starting left of the big blind, players fold, call, or raise.
      The Flop: 3 community cards are dealt, followed by another betting round.
      The Turn: a 4th community card, then betting.
      The River: the 5th and final community card, then final betting.
      Showdown: remaining players reveal their hands — the best 5-card hand wins the pot. If everyone but one player folds at any point, that player wins the pot immediately without a showdown.

      Betting actions

      Fold: give up your hand and any chance at the pot.
      Check: pass the action with no bet, only allowed if no one has bet yet this round.
      Call: match the current bet to stay in the hand.
      Bet / Raise: increase the amount others must match.
      All-in: bet every chip you have left. If you're all-in for less than the full bet, a side pot is created for the remaining players.

      This app's house rules

      • 2–8 players per room, seated by whoever holds the room code.
      • Seating and turn order reshuffle randomly before every new hand.
      • The host can kick any player from the lobby or mid-game; a kicked player is notified immediately.
      • Leaving mid-hand folds you automatically and skips you for future hands.
      • Joining mid-hand puts you in spectator mode until the next hand deals; spectators don't appear on the scoreboard.
      • The host can cancel the current hand and immediately deal a new one with the New Round button — all bets from the cancelled hand are refunded.
